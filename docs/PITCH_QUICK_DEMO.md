# SIGNAL — 3-minute hackathon pitch outline

**0:00–0:30 / Problem**
ACAPS has strong published risk analysis, but local expert knowledge can get lost in informal conversations. Experts lack time and have legitimate anonymity concerns.

**0:30–1:00 / Idea**
SIGNAL enables a short, private first-round assessment, followed by moderated anonymous group feedback and optional forecast revision.

**1:00–2:30 / Live demo**
Open R1043 (Chad). Show that 4/5 synthetic experts have already contributed. Sign in as `FIELD-05`, enter an independent event probability and missing factor, submit. Switch to `ACAPS-DEMO`, show aggregated disagreement and source context, then click **Release anonymous feedback**. Explain that experts can optionally revise; then click **Approve safe aggregate**. Open Public output to show the deliberately limited published summary.

**2:30–3:00 / Why it matters**
ACAPS can collect independent, time-efficient feedback from small trusted panels, preserve human moderation, and eventually evaluate expert forecast performance without turning the expert experience into a chatbot.

**Honesty notes**
- Official ACAPS risk record; all expert identities and submissions synthetic.
- Event forecast question is illustrative and requires ACAPS approval.
- Current code is not production-anonymous; real pilot needs a threat model and stronger authentication/ privacy controls.
- Do not promise that the prototype predicts real crises or achieves verified accuracy.

**Organizers' brief** asks for 3 minutes; the general participant guide mentions 5. Confirm at the venue.
